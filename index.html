I’m Guido, a Rigging TD based in Barcelona, with a foundation in Animation and VFX. I work where character performance meets the systems that make it possible.

My focus is on creature and character rigs, deformation, and Python tools that make everyday production a little more intuitive. I enjoy solving the intricate problems behind a simple, expressive movement.
